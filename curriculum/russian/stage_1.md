# Russian Stage 1 — 100 Essential Everyday Words & Expressions

## Goal

Stage 1 is designed to get a complete beginner communicating quickly **without requiring Cyrillic first**.

For every item, learn in this order:

1. **Meaning**
2. **Romanized Russian**
3. **Say it aloud**
4. **Recognize the Cyrillic**
5. **Use it in a tiny sentence**

Romanization is a bridge, not the final destination. During Stage 1, keep both the Latin and Cyrillic forms visible.

## Suggested Study Schedule

- **Day 1:** Items 1–20
- **Day 2:** Items 21–40
- **Day 3:** Items 41–60
- **Day 4:** Items 61–80
- **Day 5:** Items 81–100
- **Day 6:** Review all 100 + speaking drills
- **Day 7:** Self-test + mini conversations

Aim for recognition first. You do not need perfect grammar yet.

# 10 Sentence Patterns to Master

These patterns let you combine the vocabulary instead of memorizing isolated words.

### 1. Я хочу + noun / action
**Ya khochu...** — I want...

- Я хочу кофе. — Ya khochu kofe. — I want coffee.

### 2. Мне нужно + noun / action
**Mne nuzhno...** — I need...

- Мне нужна помощь. — Mne nuzhna pomoshch. — I need help.

### 3. Где + place?
**Gde...?** — Where is...?

- Где туалет? — Gde tualet? — Where is the restroom?

### 4. Что это?
**Chto eto?** — What is this?

Use this constantly when learning new objects.

### 5. Я не понимаю
**Ya ne ponimayu.** — I don't understand.

### 6. Вы говорите + language?
**Vy govorite...?** — Do you speak...?

- Вы говорите по-английски? — Do you speak English?

### 7. Я из + place
**Ya iz...** — I'm from...

### 8. Меня зовут + name
**Menya zovut...** — My name is...

### 9. Я люблю + noun / action
**Ya lyublyu...** — I love / like...

- Я люблю кофе. — I like coffee.

### 10. Можно + noun / action?
**Mozhno...?** — May I? / Is it possible?

This is one of the most useful Russian conversation patterns.

# Stage 1 Speaking Drills

## Drill A — Instant Recall

Look only at the English and say the Russian aloud.

Examples:

- thank you → спасибо
- where? → где?
- I want... → я хочу...
- today → сегодня
- help → помощь

Do 20 at a time.

## Drill B — Russian Recognition

Look at the Cyrillic and say:

1. what it means;
2. the romanized form;
3. the word aloud.

The goal is to slowly make Cyrillic feel familiar without formally studying the alphabet yet.

## Drill C — Five-Second Rule

For each English prompt, give yourself five seconds to answer in Russian.

If you cannot answer within five seconds, mark it for review.

## Drill D — Build Tiny Sentences

Use the 100 items to create short combinations:

- Я хочу чай. — I want tea.
- Мне нужна помощь. — I need help.
- Где ресторан? — Where is the restaurant?
- Я не понимаю. — I don't understand.
- Это хорошо. — This is good.
- Я работаю сегодня. — I work today.

Perfect grammar is not the priority yet. Fast recognition and communication are.

# Stage 1 Mini Conversations

## Conversation 1 — Meeting Someone

**A:** Привет!  
*Privet!*  
Hi!

**B:** Привет!  
*Privet!*  
Hi!

**A:** Меня зовут Алекс.  
*Menya zovut Aleks.*  
My name is Alex.

**B:** Меня зовут Анна.  
*Menya zovut Anna.*  
My name is Anna.

**A:** Как дела?  
*Kak dela?*  
How are you?

**B:** Хорошо, спасибо.  
*Khorosho, spasibo.*  
Good, thank you.

## Conversation 2 — Asking for Help

**A:** Извините.  
*Izvinite.*  
Excuse me.

**B:** Да?  
*Da?*  
Yes?

**A:** Где туалет?  
*Gde tualet?*  
Where is the restroom?

**B:** Там, направо.  
*Tam, napravo.*  
There, to the right.

**A:** Спасибо!  
*Spasibo!*  
Thank you!

## Conversation 3 — When You Don't Understand

**A:** Вы говорите по-английски?  
*Vy govorite po-angliyski?*  
Do you speak English?

**B:** Немного.  
*Nemnogo.*  
A little.

**A:** Я не понимаю. Повторите, пожалуйста.  
*Ya ne ponimayu. Povtorite, pozhaluysta.*  
I don't understand. Repeat, please.

# Stage 1 Self-Test

You are ready to move on when you can:

- [ ] Recognize at least **80 of the 100** items from Russian → English.
- [ ] Produce at least **70 of the 100** from English → Russian.
- [ ] Say the 10 sentence patterns without looking.
- [ ] Introduce yourself.
- [ ] Ask where something is.
- [ ] Ask someone to repeat themselves.
- [ ] Say that you understand or do not understand.
- [ ] Ask for something you want.
- [ ] Ask for something you need.
- [ ] Complete the three mini conversations with minimal help.

## Target

Do **not** wait for 100% mastery.

Once you can recall roughly **80% comfortably**, begin Stage 2 while continuing to review weak Stage 1 words.
